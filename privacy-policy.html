The Fetch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The Fetch ("we", "our") operates The Fetch app and its connected social media accounts, including the Facebook Page "The Fetch", the Instagram account @thefetch.news, and our TikTok account.

What we collect

Our app connects only to our own social media accounts through official Meta and TikTok APIs. When you interact with our Pages or accounts (for example, by following, liking, commenting, or messaging), the platforms may share with us the information they provide to Page and account owners, such as your public profile name, comments, and messages you send us.

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect payment information.

How we use information

We use platform-provided information only to operate our accounts: publishing content, responding to messages and comments, and reviewing audience analytics provided by the platforms.

Data retention and deletion

Information received through platform APIs is retained only as long as needed to operate our accounts. To request deletion of data associated with you, contact us at masyed656@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.
